# Event 1 Report: FCAJ Knowledge Sharing Session

## Summary

I attended this session to listen and learn, and I left with more than I came with. This week, the FCAJ team organized a knowledge-sharing event that reminded me why continuous learning is one of the strongest investments we can make—both individually and as a team.

## Speakers

- [**Khang Nguyen**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/khangck/)
- [**Thịnh Nguyễn**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/thinhnguyen1211/)
- [**Long Hoàng**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/huynhhoanglong1812003/)
- [**Thao Nguyen Phuong**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/thao-ngph/)

## What I learned

- Each speaker shared their learning methods, frameworks, and mindset shifts that shape how they acquire and apply knowledge.
- The session showed the power of diverse perspectives in a room: different experiences, different approaches, one shared goal.
- The most impressive part was not a single insight—it was the collective energy itself.

## Why it mattered

- The group was curious and willing to be vulnerable about what they did not know.
- Everyone was ready to support each other, which is the essence of a team built for the long run.
- Learning does not end when you leave the classroom; it becomes deeper when you surround yourself with people who challenge your thinking.

## Takeaways for the team

- Treat growth as a shared responsibility.
- Embrace curiosity, openness, and mutual support.
- Keep building more sessions like this to strengthen our team culture.

## Personal reflection

I am proud to be part of a team that values development as a collective responsibility. This event reinforced that when we learn together, we grow together.

## Thank you

Special thanks to our speakers for making this gathering possible and inspiring us to keep learning.
